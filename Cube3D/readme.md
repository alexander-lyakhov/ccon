# 3D Cube for `windows` console

[Download](https://github.com/alexander-lyakhov/ccon/tree/master/cube3d/download/)

|Keyboard shortcuts             | Action
|-------------------------------|--------
| `Up`, `Down`, `Left`, `Right` | Camera view
| `A`, `D`, `W`, `S`            | Move camera left, right, forward, backward
| `J`, `K`, `H`, `L`            | Move cube left, right, up, down
| `Space`                       | Toggle cube rotation on / off
| `I`                           | Toggle info
| `T`                           | Toggle texture on / off
| `Q`, `Esc`                    | Quit
